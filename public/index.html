The connected engineering workflow

Engineering software built around your workflow.

Solvra Hydraulics is developing a Windows desktop engineering workbench that brings geographic network construction, steady-state hydraulic analysis, engineering studies and clear result visualization into one practical environment. Explore network operating conditions, investigate constraints and compare alternatives with the system as a whole in view.

01 / Model

Model the network.

Construct interconnected pipelines and equipment in a georeferenced workspace, supported by GIS data and map-based editing.

gas and liquids.

Natural gas gathering and transmission, crude oil gathering and transfer, and produced-water pipeline systems share a need for reliable, understandable network analysis. Solvra is being developed to support steady-state gas and single-phase liquid modeling in a consistent engineering environment.
